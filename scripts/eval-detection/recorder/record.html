Landmark fixture recorder

For the person tuning the detector. Not part of the app: it is served only by the Vite dev server and is never built into the site.

What is kept: numbers only, 21 hand landmarks and the face-mesh landmarks the rules read, five times a second. No video, no pictures, no sound is recorded or sent anywhere. The camera preview stays on this page, and "Save" downloads a JSON file to your computer. A recording is still the shape of your face and hands, so keep it private unless you choose to share it.
