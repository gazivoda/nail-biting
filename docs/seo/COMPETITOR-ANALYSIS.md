# Competitor Analysis — Stop Biting

> Last updated: 2026-04-28

---

## Competitive Landscape Overview

Two distinct competitor tiers exist:

**Tier A — Direct AI-Detection App Competitors** (same product category)
**Tier B — Authoritative Content Publishers** (same search queries, different product)

Stop Biting must win against both: ranking above Tier A on product comparison queries, and eventually displacing Tier B on informational queries.

---

## Tier A: Direct App Competitors

### 1. Hands Off (handsoffapp.com)

**Threat level: HIGH**

| Attribute | Assessment |
|-----------|-----------|
| Product | Desktop app (Mac/Windows), webcam AI detection, broader BFRB focus |
| Differentiator | Broader BFRB coverage (nail biting, skin picking, hair pulling, nose picking) |
| Pricing | Similar freemium model |
| Blog content | ~5 posts (thin — major content gap vs Stop Biting) |
| SEO | ProductHunt presence, some backlinks from tech press |
| Domain Authority | Estimated DR 15–25 (early stage) |

**Content gap:** Their blog has ~5 posts with shallow treatment. Stop Biting's 40-post library is a significant depth advantage. Hands Off targets "BFRB" broadly; Stop Biting can own the narrower, higher-volume "nail biting" cluster.

**Their content topics (to watch):**
- "How to stop nail biting — 12 proven methods"
- "What is nail biting? Complete guide to onychophagia"
- "What causes nail biting?"
- "Stop nail-biting forever with on-device AI"
- "How to quit nail biting in 30 days"

**Opportunity:** Stop Biting's posts on these same topics are longer, more clinically cited, and better structured. Winning the featured snippet on "how to stop nail biting" (27K/mo) is achievable with technical improvements.

## Tier B: Authoritative Content Publishers

These sites rank for the highest-volume nail biting informational queries. Stop Biting cannot compete on Domain Authority initially but can win on depth, freshness, and user intent match.

### 1. Healthline (healthline.com)

**Threat level: HIGH for informational queries**

| Attribute | Assessment |
|-----------|-----------|
| DR | ~90 |
| Coverage | General nail biting article, causes, remedies |
| Weakness | No tool, no depth, 2–3 posts maximum on the topic |
| Opportunity | Any query where Healthline ranks position 3–10 is attackable with superior content |

---

### 2. WebMD (webmd.com)

**Threat level: HIGH for informational queries**

| Attribute | Assessment |
|-----------|-----------|
| DR | ~92 |
| Coverage | "Stop nail biting tips" — shallow, 400–600 words |
| Weakness | Monetizes via ads, no depth, no tool |
| Opportunity | Queries with "how" + specific context that WebMD's generic articles don't fully address |

---

### 3. American Academy of Dermatology (aad.org)

**Threat level: MEDIUM**

High authority, sparse content on nail biting. Single page at `/public/everyday-care/nail-care-secrets/basics/stop-biting-nails`. No blog cadence. Vulnerable to freshness displacement.

---

### 4. Psychology Today (psychologytoday.com)

**Threat level: HIGH for BFRB/OCD queries**

| Attribute | Assessment |
|-----------|-----------|
| DR | ~88 |
| Coverage | Onychophagia condition page, OCD spectrum articles |
| Weakness | No tool, generalist, infrequent updates |
| Opportunity | Specific clinical queries ("nail biting OCD", "nail biting ADHD", "onychophagia treatment") |

---

### 5. TLC Foundation for BFRBs (bfrb.org)

**Threat level: MEDIUM for clinical queries**

| Attribute | Assessment |
|-----------|-----------|
| DR | ~55 |
| Coverage | Authoritative BFRB resource, therapist directory |
| Relationship opportunity | Link target — getting linked from bfrb.org is high-value backlink |

## Positioning Summary

| Dimension | Hands Off | Stop Biting |
|-----------|----------|-------------|
| Focus | Broad BFRB | Nail biting specialist |
| Platform | Desktop | Desktop + Web PWA |
| Content depth | Shallow (5 posts) | Deep (40+ posts) |
| Privacy architecture | On-device | On-device |
| SEO footprint | Small but growing | Early but content-rich |
| Price | Similar | $2.99/mo |

**Positioning recommendation:** Stop Biting should lean into "nail biting specialist" vs Hands Off's broader BFRB positioning. The depth of the blog library supports genuine authority in the narrower niche, which will convert better for search visitors with a specific nail biting problem.
