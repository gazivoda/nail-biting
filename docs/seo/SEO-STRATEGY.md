> Last updated: 2026-04-28

---

## Business Context

**Product:** Stop Biting (stopbiting.today)
**Category:** Health SaaS / Habit Tech
**Model:** Freemium — 3-day free trial → $2.99/month or $29/year
**Differentiators:** PWA + desktop (Mac/Windows), 100% on-device AI, webcam-based detection, privacy-first architecture

**SEO Goal:** Become the authoritative content destination for nail biting (onychophagia) — owning informational, commercial, and BFRB-related queries — and convert organic readers into free trial users.

## Current State Assessment

### Strengths
- 40 blog posts published (April 2026), comprehensive topic coverage
- Strong schema markup on homepage (FAQPage, SoftwareApplication, MedicalCondition, Organization)
- robots.txt properly configured with AI crawler permissions
- llms.txt present for GEO discoverability
- Sitemap exists with core URLs

### Weaknesses
- Sitemap outdated — ~20 blog posts missing
- Sitemap slug mismatch: `onychophagia-ocd-connection` vs actual `nail-biting-ocd-connection`
- No Article schema on individual blog posts
- No author bio / Person schema (E-E-A-T gap)
- SPA architecture — blog content is client-rendered, not SSR (reduces crawlability)
- No About page / founder story
- No hub/pillar pages — all content is flat
- No backlinks (new domain)
- No Reddit, Wikipedia, or YouTube presence
- llms.txt not updated with posts published after April 7

### Opportunity Score: HIGH
The nail biting keyword cluster is underserved by dedicated tool-makers. Most top-ranking content is from general health publishers (Healthline, WebMD, AAD) with no product. Stop Biting can own the intersection of educational content + commercial intent.
