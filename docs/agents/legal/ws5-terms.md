# WS5 draft: Terms of Service changes for the photo diary, charts, milestones and the weekly email

Status: DRAFT. Not published. `src/pages/TermsPage.tsx` has not been edited. Short on purpose: these features add little to
the terms. A lawyer should decide whether they need anything at all.

## Proposed additions

### Optional features (new short section, or inside the existing description of the service)

> The charts, milestones, share pictures, photo diary and weekly summary email are optional features that you can switch on or
> off. They describe your own entries and are not medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment, and they are not a replacement
> for a therapist or doctor.

### Photo diary

> Photos in the photo diary are stored only in your browser on your device. We cannot see them and we cannot recover them. If
> you clear your browser's site data, use a private window, switch browsers or lose the device, they are gone. Keep your own copy
> of anything you want to keep. You are responsible for what you photograph with your own camera.

(The app does not offer a way to export the photos. If you add one, the privacy draft changes too.)

### Weekly summary email

> If you turn on the weekly summary email we send one email a week to the address of your account. You can turn it off in
> Settings or with the unsubscribe link in any email. It is built from the history you chose to sync and shows counts; it does
> not say why numbers change. We may change or stop sending it.

### Share pictures

> A share picture is created on your device. If you share it, you decide where it goes; we do not receive it and are not
> responsible for what others do with a picture you share.

## What was deliberately not written

- No claim that any of these features helps, reduces or stops a habit.
- No promise that photos survive: they are as durable as the browser's storage.
- No uptime or delivery promise for the email (delivery depends on the email provider and the recipient's mail system).
