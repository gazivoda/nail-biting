1. Tally. Each time you notice your hand at your mouth, make a mark in the tally box. Noticing is the point; the number will often be higher than you expect.
2. Note the moment. Tick what was going on: stress, deep focus, boredom or something else. Patterns show up after a week or two.
3. Competing response. Tick the “competing response” box if you did something else with your hands instead, such as pressing your palms flat or holding an object.
