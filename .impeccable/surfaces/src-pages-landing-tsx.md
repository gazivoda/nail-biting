# Homepage (/) surface brief

Scope: the homepage, src/pages/Landing.tsx (+ PricingSection, HeroDemo). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: adults who bite during screen work, arriving from search; tried polish/willpower.
Job: understand in seconds what it is (webcam alarm when hand reaches mouth), trust it (privacy, science), start.
Action: Start free trial (web, Google sign-in, new tab, 3 days, no card). Demo is proof, not the goal.
Must keep: live demo, HRT science + PubMed sources, privacy proof, FAQ (byte-identical to index.html JSON-LD), guide links.
Wrong if: clinical/medical, generic SaaS, long.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Explain the product the way a safety sign does: one pictogram, one second, no reading. Refuses the wellness-app hero (gradient, phone mockup, feature cards) and the long editorial essay it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: ISO 7010 sign language. Enamel plates with rounded corners; yellow warning triangle reserved for the alarm alone; blue circular "mandatory" plates for what you do (start, allow camera, palms flat); forest-green square "safe condition" plates for privacy. Black pictogram ink on sign-white. Overpass (Highway Gothic heir) heavy for headings, regular for body; Overpass Mono only for sign codes and timestamps. Section codes W01/M01-M03/E01 double as nav index.

STORY: Visitor reads the headline and the warning plate and knows it is an alarm for hand-to-mouth. Three blue steps show exactly how to start. Science and privacy are two compact plates of proof. Price and FAQ close. They press the blue trial sign.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 6/12: h1 "Stop biting your nails.", a one-sentence plain explanation, blue trial sign-button, offer line "3 days free · no card · then $2.99/month". Right 6/12: large yellow warning plate with the hand-to-mouth pictogram and dashed threshold ring, "Try the live demo" mounted in it. Mobile: headline, plate, button.

FORM: Safety Sign System, list position 4 of 7 (roll), seed key 79f8cbca. Signature interaction: the pictogram hand rises, crosses the real threshold ring and the plate flashes; the live demo replaces it and only a real catch turns the plate yellow. Raises: sign-code index (Teletext), only the live element glows (Neon), ring = real threshold (Ink), tabular timestamps (Daylight).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
